## Summary

Age of the Codex does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. Your progress is saved only on your device.

## What the game stores on your device

- **Game saves**: your story progress, choices, inventory and the history log, stored in the app's private storage on your device.
- **Settings**: language, volume, screen shake and similar preferences, stored on your device.

This data stays on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game (unless your device's own backup service keeps app data).

## Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details. The store tells the game which items your account owns so they can be unlocked and restored; the game stores a purchase identifier on your device to record the unlock.

## What we do not do

- No account or sign-in.
- No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
- No analytics, tracking or crash reporting (at the time of this version).
- No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access.
- No data sold or shared with third parties.

## Children

The game is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version starts collecting data (for example crash reports), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be noted here.
